# Product Overview

Raj's Cafe is a luxury dining experience application featuring an artisanal coffee shop with gourmet cuisine. The project consists of:

## Main Components
- **Customer-facing website**: Showcases the cafe's menu, atmosphere, and allows reservations
- **Admin panel**: Complete management system for orders, reservations, menu items, and content

## Key Features
- **Menu Management**: Dynamic menu with categories, pricing, and availability controls
- **Reservation System**: Table booking with confirmation workflow
- **Order Management**: Real-time order processing and status tracking
- **Contact System**: Customer inquiries and communication management
- **Content Management**: Blog posts, SEO settings, and design customization
- **Analytics**: Revenue tracking, activity monitoring, and business insights

## Brand Identity
- Warm, artisanal cafe aesthetic with luxury positioning
- Focus on sustainability, community roots, and exceptional service
- Color scheme centered around cream, amber/gold, and warm browns
- Typography uses serif fonts for headings (DM Serif Display, Playfair Display) and sans-serif for body text (Lato, Nunito)

## Target Users
- **Cafe owners/managers**: Using the admin panel for daily operations
- **Customers**: Browsing menu, making reservations, and placing orders
- **Staff**: Managing day-to-day operations and customer service
